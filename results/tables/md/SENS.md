Excluding the suspect record (134 records; all folds, tuning and scores repeated for the grouped protocols) did not change the conclusions. The R² of the power law with separate groups was 0.96 at 55 m with and without the record, 0.88 and 0.91 under leave-one-bed-out and 0.87 and 0.89 under leave-one-soil-out; for composite-input XGBoost the corresponding values were 0.55 and 0.59 (55 m), 0.66 and 0.73 (leave-one-bed-out) and 0.24 and 0.35 (leave-one-soil-out). The ranking of the model/input combinations was the same. Some estimates based on few records are unstable, e.g. the R² of composite-input SVR for a new bed at 55 m (three test records per fold) changed from 0.64 to 0.09, which is a further reason to avoid ranking the models on this dataset.
